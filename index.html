(01)The centre

When evening falls and the day's copybooks close, Centre Souifi turns its lights back on. Soutien scolaire from primary to Terminale, languages that open the world, teachers with years of classroom behind them — and a personal follow-up for every pupil, because behind every report card there is a child we know by name. We don't replace school — we make it finally make sense. And one house rule: nobody is asked what mark they got yesterday — only what they understood tonight.
