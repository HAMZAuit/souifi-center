(01)The idea

After six in the evening, Kenitra changes gear. The offices empty, the traffic softens, and on Avenue Mohammed V one building turns its lights back on. Night Academy is built for people who spend their days working for others and their evenings working on themselves. Teachers who ship real projects. Classrooms that smell of mint tea. And one house rule: nobody asks what you do by day — only what you're becoming by night.
